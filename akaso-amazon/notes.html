Universal Glue

润滑 / 推进 / 缓冲万能句
— Warm agreement 认同
"That's a really good point."
"That makes a lot of sense — thank you."
"I love that. Let's build on it."
— Buy time 争取思考
"Let me think about that for a second."
"Good question — let me give you the honest version."
— Make sure I get it 确认理解
"Just to make sure I understand — you mean…?"
"Can I check I've got that right?"
— Softly disagree 温和不同意
"I see it slightly differently — can I share how?"
"I hear you. One thing I'd add…"
— Steer / park 引导 · 暂放
"Can we come back to that one in a moment?"
"Let me park that and make sure we cover…"
— Land it 收口 · 下一步
"So, to summarize what we've agreed…"
"What would be the best next step from your side?"
"Who should own this on each side?"
