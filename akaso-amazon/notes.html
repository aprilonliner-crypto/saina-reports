Opening — Codorníu

Two Brands

不重叠 不打价
"Quick clarity on our two brands — they often get lumped together."
"AKASO = sports & outdoor imaging. Dragon Touch = smart-home display — calendars, cloud frames. Different customer, different shelf."
"So — no overlap, no price conflict. Never the same keyword or buyer."
03

1P vs 3P

核心立场 · 稳住
"We don't see 1P and 3P as either/or. They're two different capabilities."
"3P carries validation & incubation. 1P carries the scaled retailing of mature products. It's a lifecycle thing."
"A product 'graduates' into 1P once volume, returns and pricing are stable — not before."
04

New Launches

"H2 ones are still incubating — I'd rather validate them properly than over-commit."
05

Beyond Net PPM

软中带硬 · 谈钱
"One thing I'd gently push on — let's not optimize only for the opening Net PPM."
"Net PPM is the entry ticket. What we should jointly optimize is a sustainable contribution margin — returns, turns, price stability."
"Get that right and it's a healthier business for both sides — not just a good-looking first number."
